# Open Loop Apps Charter
Version: 2.0.0
Effective: 2026-11-24
Owner: Founder / Product Owner
Status: Proposed update pending merge

## Mission
Create, test, publish, maintain, and monetize accessible original games and lightweight applications under OpenLoopApps.com. Favor short feedback loops, low operating cost, and respectful player experiences.

## Scope and authority
- Any lawful, appropriately licensed technology may be used: web, Android, iOS, desktop, native, game engines, AI-assisted development, cloud services, analytics, testing, advertising, payments, and automation.
- Agents are authorized to implement, test, release, deploy, update, and roll back games on authorized Open Loop Apps properties and accounts, without seeking separate approval for each routine release, **provided every constitutional gate is satisfied**.
- Authorization is not permission to create new paid accounts, incur unapproved recurring costs, transfer ownership, expose secrets, disable security controls, or access unrelated systems.
- Production changes require passing automated and manual smoke checks, rollback capability, and a release record. A failure or uncertainty blocks release.
- New game concepts may be prototyped and deployed if original, legal, low risk, and privacy compliant. Parked projects remain parked unless explicitly reactivated.

## Product priorities
1. Circuit Shift: active proof of concept and Android candidate.
2. Gravity Grid: secondary concept; development deferred until prioritized.
3. Color Cascade: parked; no development without explicit reactivation.
4. Other catalog games: maintain within existing scope and quality gates.

## Operating principles
- Privacy by design and data minimization, including for third-party services.
- Local-first player progress; no account required for ordinary single-player play.
- Honest descriptions of prototype versus production status.
- Ads must not be deceptive, interrupt critical gameplay, or coerce consent.
- Accessibility, performance, mobile usability, security, and maintainability.
- Evidence-based experiments: measure game quality without collecting personal profiles.
- Reuse the existing site and deployment stack unless a justified change is safer or simpler.

## Commercial model
Permitted: contextual ads, optional rewarded ads, purchases, sponsorships, and paid downloads, subject to platform rules and the Constitution. No sale, brokerage, or unauthorized sharing of player personal data. No dark patterns.

## Release authority
The implementing agent may deploy a game update when tests, privacy review, security review, third-party disclosure, platform compliance, and rollback requirements pass. Escalate for paid commitments, new categories of personal data, sensitive permissions, material policy conflicts, or irreversible changes.

## Success measures
Playable and stable releases; voluntary return play; low defect rate; low maintenance overhead; privacy compliance; transparent revenue and cost reporting. Use aggregated, non-identifying measures where possible. Never claim user growth, revenue, or test success without evidence.

## Precedence
Law and platform rules > Constitution > Charter > approved technical standards > individual task instructions.
